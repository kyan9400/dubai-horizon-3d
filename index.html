The Tower

A quarter turn, floor by floor.

Each floor plate rotates 1.25° from the one beneath it. Over 72 levels the tower completes a 90° turn — enough to give the upper residences a corner view of the Gulf, and to let the facade shade itself through the hottest hours.

Vertical fins in brushed champagne aluminium are angled to the sun path: closed to the south at noon, open to the west at dusk.

Location

On the water, ten minutes from everything.
